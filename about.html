About Trusted Care

Welcome To

Trusted Care

At Trusted Care, every individual gets the care they need in a frum and warm environment. We evaluate, diagnose, and treat a multitude of psychiatric conditions, including:

• Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)

• Anxiety

• Depression

• Obsessive Compulsive Disorder (OCD)

• Bipolar

We specialize in treating children, adolescents, and young adults. Our care is centered on giving every patient the specialized care they need and deserve. We understand that while patients may display similar symptoms, no two patients are the same.

Getting medication can be a difficult process; at Trusted Care we guide you through the process in a warm compassionate environment.
