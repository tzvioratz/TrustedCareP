--- --- {% include issueheader.html title="Bipolar" desc='Bipolar Disorder is a mood condition characterized by shifts between periods of depression and periods of elevated mood, increased energy, or irritability known as mania or hypomania. These mood changes are more intense and long-lasting than normal ups and downs and can affect judgment, sleep, motivation, thinking, and daily functioning. During depressive phases, a person may feel sad, exhausted, or hopeless, while manic or hypomanic phases may involve racing thoughts, decreased need for sleep, impulsive decisions, or a sense of being unusually energized or driven. With proper diagnosis, treatment, and support, many people with bipolar disorder live stable, fulfilling, and meaningful lives.' %} {% include accordion.html number='1' title='Bipolar I Disorder' main='Bipolar I Disorder involves episodes of depression along with at least one episode of mania, which is a period of extremely elevated or irritable mood, increased energy, and impulsive behavior that may significantly affect daily life. With proper diagnosis, ongoing support, and effective treatment, many people with Bipolar I are able to stabilize their mood and live meaningful, productive lives.' %} {% include accordion.html number='2' title='Bipolar II Disorder' main='Bipolar II Disorder includes episodes of depression along with hypomania, a milder form of elevated mood and increased energy that is noticeable but not as severe as full mania. With compassionate care and the right treatment plan, individuals with Bipolar II can achieve stability, emotional balance, and improved quality of life.' %} {% include accordion.html number='3' title='Cyclothymic Disorder (Cyclothymia)' main='Cyclothymic Disorder is a milder but longer-lasting form of bipolar-related mood changes that involves ongoing shifts between periods of low mood and periods of elevated mood that don’t reach full depressive or manic intensity.
