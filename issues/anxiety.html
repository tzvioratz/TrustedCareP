--- --- {% include issueheader.html title="Anxiety" desc='Anxiety is a common mental health condition marked by excessive worry, fear, or nervousness that can feel difficult to control and may interfere with daily life. People may experience racing thoughts, restlessness, tension, irritability, difficulty concentrating, or trouble sleeping, along with physical symptoms such as rapid heartbeat, shortness of breath, sweating, or stomach discomfort. While everyone feels anxious at times, anxiety becomes a concern when it is persistent, overwhelming, or begins to limit functioning. With proper support and treatment, anxiety is highly manageable, and individuals can regain calm, confidence, and control in their lives.' %} {% include accordion.html number='1' title='Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)' main='Generalized Anxiety Disorder involves ongoing worry and tension about everyday life, even when there may not be an obvious reason for concern. It can affect daily life, but with the right support and treatment, most people experience meaningful relief.' %} {% include accordion.html number='2' title='Social Anxiety Disorder' main='Social Anxiety Disorder is more than shyness—it’s an intense fear of being judged, embarrassed, or negatively evaluated in social or performance situations. Many people with social anxiety improve significantly with therapy and support.' %} {% include accordion.html number='3' title='Panic Disorder' main='Panic Disorder involves repeated, sudden panic attacks—surges of intense fear that may include a racing heart, shortness of breath, dizziness, or a feeling of losing control. Effective treatments are available, and many people learn to reduce or stop panic attacks entirely.' %} {% include accordion.html number='1' title='Illness Anxiety Disorder (Health Anxiety)' main='Illness Anxiety Disorder involves persistent worry about having or developing a serious medical condition, even when exams and tests are reassuring. With proper treatment, most people can gradually regain confidence and independence.' %} {% include accordion.html number='1' title='Specific Phobias' main='Specific Phobias involve strong fear of a particular object or situation—such as flying, heights, animals, or medical procedures—that leads to anxiety and avoidance.
