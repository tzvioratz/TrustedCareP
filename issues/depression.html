Depression can make even simple tasks feel overwhelming and may impact relationships, work, and overall quality of life. It is not a sign of weakness; it is a real and treatable condition. With compassionate care and effective treatment, many people experience significant improvement and regain a sense of purpose, connection, and joy.' %} {% include accordion.html number='2' title='Persistent Depressive Disorder (Dysthymia)' main='Persistent Depressive Disorder is a longer-lasting form of depression in which a person experiences low mood, sadness, or emotional heaviness for most days over at least two years.
