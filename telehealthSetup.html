Prepare for Your Virtual Appointment

To ensure your appointment runs smoothly, please take a few minutes to prepare your device and environment before joining your session.

Step 1: Test Your Device

✔ Make sure you are using a device with a camera and microphone
✔ Use a modern browser (Chrome recommended)
✔ Allow access to your camera and microphone when prompted

Step 2: Check Audio & Video

✔ Speak and confirm your microphone is working
✔ Check that your camera is showing your face clearly
✔ Adjust lighting so your face is visible

Step 3: Internet Connection

✔ Use a stable internet connection
✔ Wi-Fi is fine, but wired connection is best if available
✔ Close other apps or downloads to avoid lag

Step 4: Choose a Good Environment

✔ Sit in a quiet, private space
✔ Minimize background noise
✔ Avoid interruptions during your session

Quick Checklist

  • Device charged or plugged in
  • Camera and microphone working
  • Stable internet connection
  • Quiet and private location
Having trouble?
If you experience issues joining the session, try refreshing the page, restarting your browser, or switching devices.
